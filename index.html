Escape the Psych Clinic

Dr. Quirk has locked you in her very messy clinic. Search each room, read the clues, then name the disorder to open the door. Five rooms, about 10 minutes.

Learning outcome

You will be able to differentiate the types of disorders: major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder (both mood disorders) and schizophrenia.

How to play

Goal

Escape all 5 rooms. In each room, inspect every object to find clues, then try the locked door. The door asks a question about a character. Pick the answer that fits the situation.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: tap objects, the door and answer buttons.
  • Keyboard: press 1 2 3 to inspect the objects and the door, 1–4 to choose an answer, Esc to close a clue, Tab and Enter to move and press buttons.

Scoring

  • Correct on the 1st try: 20 points. 2nd try: 10. 3rd try or more: 5.
  • A wrong answer never ends the game. Read why it is wrong, then try again.
  • Speed does not matter. The clock is only a pacing guide, and it never changes your score.
  • Maximum score: 100.
